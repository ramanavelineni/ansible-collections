# Code of conduct

This applies to everything in this repository: issues, pull requests, reviews
and security advisories.

## What is expected

- Be respectful. Disagree with the idea, not the person.
- Assume good intent, and say plainly when something isn't clear.
- Keep to the subject of the issue or pull request.
- Accept that a request can be declined; this is a one-person project with a
  narrow scope.

## What is not acceptable

- Insults, personal attacks or harassment of any kind.
- Discriminatory language or jokes about who someone is.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Continuing after being asked to stop.

## Reporting

- In a public thread, mention the maintainer, `@ramanavelineni`.
- To report something privately to GitHub, use **Report content** in the
  `...` menu of the comment, issue or pull request.

## What happens

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, lock a thread, or block an account
from the repository, depending on how serious the behaviour is and whether it
is repeated.
